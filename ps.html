Difference Between AI's

Explore the strengths and limitations of popular AI assistants.

🤖 ChatGPT

BOONS

• Listens carefully
• Responds with accurate and understandable information
• Respects your pace and feelings

BANS

• Doesn't browse the live internet by default
• Can sometimes give incorrect information
• Cannot replace real professionals

✨ Gemini

BOONS

• Helpful for learning and research
• Can work with different types of information
• Useful for everyday questions

BANS

• Can sometimes make mistakes
• Important information should be checked

💻 Copilot

BOONS

• Helpful for coding
• Useful for productivity
• Can explain programming concepts

BANS

• Code should always be checked
• May sometimes provide incorrect suggestions

🧠 Claude

BOONS

• Good for writing and reasoning
• Gives clear and detailed responses
• Useful for analysing information

BANS

• Can make mistakes
• Not a replacement for professional advice

🌐 Meta AI

BOONS

• Useful for everyday conversations
• Easy to access through Meta platforms
• Helpful for simple questions

BANS

• Responses can sometimes be inaccurate
• Important information should be verified
